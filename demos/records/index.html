What building this taught me

  • A document containing a field does not make it that document. A birth certificate has the CURP printed on it, and the naive classifier took it for a CURP document. Classification goes by the document’s own markers, not by the fields that appear inside.
  • A valid document may not be yours. A genuine ID belonging to someone else passes any format check. That is why cross-validation compares the extracted data against the taxpayer record before accepting.
  • Validity dates matter as much as content. A two-year-old proof of address is a correct document and a useless one at the same time.
  • When the text cannot be extracted — a photo or a scan with no text layer — the system says so and offers manual entry, instead of failing silently.
Written from scratch in JavaScript, no libraries. In the real system, text extraction uses smalot/pdfparser on the PDF text layer, with OCR as the fallback for scanned documents.
