Trip scheduling calendar

Interactive demo · vanilla JavaScript, no libraries

A recreation of the scheduling module I built for a transportation system in production. The original is private client code, so I wrote this one from scratch, with made-up data, so it can actually be used.

Drag an event to another day to reschedule it; a click without movement opens its details. The target day highlights before you drop.

Rules this demo enforces

  • A trip cannot be moved to a past date; the attempt is rejected and the event stays on its original day.
  • Dragging uses Pointer Events and works with a mouse or touch. Movement greater than 4 pixels counts as a drag.
  • The target day highlights green or red before you release; valid events move directly to that day.
  • A click without movement opens a read-only information card with the event’s essential details.
  • Event colors are configured by type in the right panel; client, unit, and driver filters combine.
  • All data is synthetic, and selected colors are stored locally in this browser.
