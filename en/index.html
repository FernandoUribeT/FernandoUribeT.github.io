Document validation with OCR

Vanilla JavaScript · classification and cross-validation

A supplier uploads documents and the system extracts the text, decides which document it is, and validates that it belongs to them. Switch between naive and hardened validation and watch the difference live.

The first three documents are the real defects I found in production: a birth certificate classified as a CURP document because it has one printed on it, a tax filing receipt taken for proof of address, and a genuine ID belonging to someone else.

Vehicle inspection with signature and lockout

Vanilla JavaScript · canvas · a module I am the sole author of

The driver checks the unit item by item and signs on screen. Mark a critical fault and the unit locks itself, without anyone having to report it.

An incomplete or unsigned inspection cannot be closed either: a half-done inspection saved as complete creates false confidence, which is worse than having no inspection at all.

Fleet QR identifiers

JavaScript, no libraries · QR encoder written from scratch

The QR on that page comes from no library: I wrote the whole encoder, with Reed–Solomon correction, block interleaving and all eight masks. Put a logo in the centre and move the correction level to see how much damage it survives.

The verdict is not a simulation: it reads the canvas back, counts the codewords the logo genuinely damages and compares them against what each block can correct. I checked it against zxing-cpp, the decoder behind phone scanners, across all 76 combinations of level and logo size.

fleet-maintenance

Laravel 13 · PHP 8.5 · PHPUnit · SQLite

A multi-tenant service that tells a dispatcher which units can go out today and which are held back for service. Built to a single rule: business rules do not depend on the framework.

The 12 domain tests run in 2 ms because they touch neither Laravel nor the database; the 24 integration tests take 1.6 s. That gap is the whole argument, and it is measured in the README.

It includes per-company isolation through a global scope that fails closed, a detail page answering 404 — not 403 — for another carrier's id, and a test that posts company_id to prove the form cannot pick its own tenant.
